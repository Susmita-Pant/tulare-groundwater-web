Tulare Basin Groundwater Explorer

1 · Choose what to map

Absolute groundwater head is the elevation of the water level (feet or metres above sea level). Cumulative head change is head in the selected year minus head in a base year: negative = water level fell, positive = rose. Pick the confined or unconfined aquifer.

2 · Move through time

Drag the Year slider or press Play. For change maps, set the Base year. The provided 1971–2023 change is a fixed-period product, so it has no year control.

3 · Click the map

Click anywhere for a hydrograph: lines are the interpolated surfaces at that 500 m cell; dots are observed wells nearby. Click a well marker to highlight its record and see its well profile (screens, depth, Corcoran Clay reference).

4 · Compare and download

Basin summary compares confined and unconfined statistics for the whole area or one subbasin. Storage change shows regional totals since 2015. Every view has download buttons (CSV, image, grid).

Regional groundwater storage change since 2015

Signed storage change in km³ since 2015 (negative = storage decrease), regional totals from storage_depletion_2015_2023.xlsx. Confined total = water expansion + matrix compaction; combined total adds the unconfined change (range: specific yield Sy = 0.1 to 0.3). Aquifer-matrix compaction is a storage loss linked to land subsidence, but these values are volumes of water, not ground-surface lowering.
